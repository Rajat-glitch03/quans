QUICK START GUIDES

Know what to add. Know what to do.

Each workflow starts with your context. Add the details you know, choose the options that fit your goal, then review the result before using it.

01
CAREER DEVELOPMENT

Using Resume Builder

  1. 1
    Enter your name and target role, then add the contact details you want shown.
  2. 2
    Fill in your summary, experience, education, skills and projects. Add certifications or achievements when relevant.
  3. 3
    Optionally paste a target job description, select Generate Resume, review the draft, then copy it or download the text file.

Helpful tip: Use real details and measurable results only when you can verify them.

02
CONTENT CREATION

Using Post Studio

  1. 1
    Describe your topic, business or idea in the context box.
  2. 2
    Choose the platform, tone, audience, goal and post length. Turn on Add CTA when you want a call to action.
  3. 3
    Optionally add your profile URL and a writing sample, then select Generate Content. Review and use the refinement options if needed.

Helpful tip: Include the key point you want people to remember and who the post is for.

03
BUSINESS DOCUMENTS

Using Proposal Generator

  1. 1
    Paste the client's project description or requirements into the Client Requirement box.
  2. 2
    Select the platform, tone and proposal length. Choose whether to include a CTA.
  3. 3
    Optionally add your portfolio or website URL and a past proposal sample, then select Generate Proposal. Review, copy or refine the result.

Helpful tip: Keep the client's scope, expected deliverables and constraints in the brief.

04
CONTENT REPURPOSING

Using Text Repurposer

  1. 1
    Paste the content you want to adapt into the main content box.
  2. 2
    Choose the source and target platforms, audience, tone, repurpose mode and output length. Set Add CTA if required.
  3. 3
    Optionally add profile context and a writing sample, then select Repurpose. Review and refine the new version.

Helpful tip: Start with complete source content and choose one clear target platform at a time.

MAKE THE MOST OF EACH TOOL

Better context. More useful drafts.

QUANS helps shape the information you provide. Your context and final review still matter.

Be clear about the goal

Say what you are creating, who it is for and what you want the reader to do or understand.

Add relevant details

Include the useful facts, requirements, examples and constraints instead of relying on a vague prompt.

Review before sharing

Check names, dates, claims, links and tone. Edit the generated draft so it accurately represents you or your business.
